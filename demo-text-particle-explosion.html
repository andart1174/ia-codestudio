How the 3D Text Particle Explosion Engine Works

Interactive Three.js BufferGeometry typography physics running at 60 FPS inside your browser.

This live WebGL showcase demonstrates how rasterized HTML5 2D canvas typography can be sampled pixel-by-pixel and transformed into thousands of real-time 3D particles rendered by Three.js. Every letter in the scene is converted into a lightweight Float32Array coordinate buffer so the GPU animates position, velocity, and RGB shimmer without creating individual DOM elements or heavy polygon meshes.

When your cursor enters the central interaction zone, the physics loop applies radial impulse vectors with subtle Z-axis depth dispersion and velocity damping. Moving your mouse through the exploded cloud applies a soft magnetic attraction field, while leaving the trigger zone smoothly interpolates every particle back to its original typographic anchor using spring easing and sinusoidal breathing motion.

Key Technical Highlights

  • GPU-accelerated Three.js Points & BufferGeometry pipeline with dynamic vertex color updates.
  • Zero external 3D model downloads — typography is procedurally sampled at runtime from an offscreen canvas.
  • Interactive hover shatter physics, cursor gravity field, and smooth spring reassembly at 60 FPS.
