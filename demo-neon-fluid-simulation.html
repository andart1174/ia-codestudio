How the Real-Time Neon Fluid & Metaball Physics Engine Works

Interactive 60 FPS fluid droplet dynamics and organic surface tension built with pure client-side JavaScript.

The Neon Fluid Simulation showcases continuous metaball physics rendered directly on an HTML5 Canvas with custom glow compositing and real-time vector attraction. Each glowing neon droplet stores position, velocity, radius, and chromatic hue, interacting smoothly with neighboring drops and viewport boundaries to produce organic liquid-metal merging behavior.

Clicking anywhere inside the viewport injects new pressurized neon droplets into the simulation while hovering creates a directional gravitational attractor that pulls nearby fluid clusters toward your pointer. Because the entire physics solver runs locally on the client with zero server round-trips, input latency stays near zero across both desktop trackpads and touchscreens.
