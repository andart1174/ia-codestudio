What Is a 3D Product Viewer?

A 3D product viewer is an interactive widget embedded on your website that lets visitors rotate, zoom, and inspect a product from every angle. Unlike flat product photos, a 3D viewer creates an immersive experience that builds trust and drives conversions.

Modern 3D product viewers use WebGL technology — the same engine powering video games in browsers — to render real-time 3D graphics without any plugins or downloads.
