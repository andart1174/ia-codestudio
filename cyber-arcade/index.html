CYBER ARCADE 3D — OFFICIAL PILOT GUIDE & RULES

PC / Laptop Controls (Keyboard & Mouse)

  • W / A / S / D or ↑ / ← / ↓ / → — Steer / Move Craft & Aim
  • SPACE / ENTER — Fire Plasma Lasers / Gatling / Slice
  • E / M — Fire Lock-on Homing Missile
  • Q / F — Overdrive Mega-Beam / Energy Sword
  • SHIFT — Turbo Boost / Nitro Speed (400 km/h)
  • C — Toggle 1st Person Cockpit / 3rd Person
  • V — Toggle Cyber Night-Vision Scanner
  • ESC / P — Pause / Exit to Hub

Mobile & Tablet Controls (Touch & Gyro)

  • 🕹️ Left Joystick — Touch & drag anywhere on the left to steer smoothly.
  • 🔥 FIRE — Tap to shoot dual lasers / Gatling rounds.
  • 🎯 MISSILE — Tap to launch auto-homing explosive missiles.
  • ⚡ BEAM — Fires screen-clearing Overdrive Mega-Beam!
  • ⏩ BOOST — Hold for hyper-speed acceleration.
  • 📱 360° GYRO (AR) — Physically turn phone in your room to aim at aliens!
