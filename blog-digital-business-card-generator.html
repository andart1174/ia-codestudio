What Is a Digital Business Card?

A digital business card is an electronic version of the traditional paper business card. Instead of a physical card that gets lost in a desk drawer, it lives on your phone, in your email signature, on your website, or encoded in a QR code that anyone can scan. It carries the same core information — name, title, company, contact details — but adds features that paper simply cannot match.

The most common format for digital business cards is the vCard (also called a .vcf file). This is an industry-standard file format supported natively by every smartphone — iPhone, Android, and everything in between. When someone receives your vCard, a single tap adds you to their contacts. No typing. No errors. No lost card.
