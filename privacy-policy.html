Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the browser extension OmniConvert Pro - Ultimate Dev Toolkit (and associated web utility pages hosted on ia-codestudio.com).

1. Data Collection and Usage

OmniConvert Pro is designed as a 100% offline-first application.

  • No Data Collection: We do not collect, capture, store, or monitor any personal data, text inputs, files, code snippets, tokens, keys, or configurations that you process through the tool.
  • Local Execution: All conversion, formatting, minification, encoding, and decoding operations are executed entirely on your local device using client-side JavaScript.
  • No Transmission: No data typed or loaded into this extension is ever transmitted over the network to our servers or any third-party services.

2. Permissions

This extension does not request or require any special browser permissions (such as access to tab history, storage, or external websites) to perform its core functions.

3. Third-Party Services

The extension does not integrate with third-party analytical engines, tracking pixels, or advertisement trackers. Your usage remains completely private.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at our official portal: ia-codestudio.com.
