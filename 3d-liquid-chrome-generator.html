🖨️ How to Export 3MF Multicolor for 3D Printing

Follow these 3 easy steps to turn any interactive 3D model into a physical multicolor 3D print:

1

Select and Customize Your Model

Launch the studio, select a compartment (e.g. Cyber-Mecha 4D or Tourbillon Watch), and use the control sliders to adjust dimensions, armor complexity, or gear ratios.

2

Click Export 3MF (Multicolor Ready)

In the right toolbar, click the 'Export 3MF' button. The built-in 3MF generator compiles all distinct parts with their corresponding material hex colors into standard 3MF XML containers.

3

Open in Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, or OrcaSlicer

Drag the downloaded .3mf file into your slicer. The software will instantly identify each colored component, mapping them to your multi-material AMS / MMU slots for seamless printing.
